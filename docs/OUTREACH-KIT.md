# Outreach kit: getting the first 100 creators

*November 2026. Messages, replies and posts for the founding-creator push in [GO-TO-MARKET.md](GO-TO-MARKET.md), sections 2 and 8. Copy, change the bracketed parts, and send. Track every name in [outreach-tracker.csv](outreach-tracker.csv).*

## Before you send anything

1. **Seed the site first.** Get 25–30 real works on it (yours, friends', anyone who says yes early), then turn on `HIDE_DEMO_CONTENT`. Every message sends people to the site, and demo content kills trust on first click.
2. **Send from an account with real posts.** A blank Instagram or a brand-new account looks like spam, and Instagram limits new accounts that send lots of similar messages. Your personal account, with a line in the bio about building Unprompters, works best.
3. **Know what to promise.** Only offer what's real today:

| Offer | Status | What to say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A page showing your work and how it was made, with an AI score and range | Built | Say it freely |
| Free to post, no need to sell | Built | Say it freely |
| People can ask you about buying, even with no price | Built (Ask the creator) | Say it freely |
| Sell with your own payment link, we take nothing | Built | Say it freely |
| Embeddable score badge for your site | Built for **items for sale only**, and it's HTML, so it can't go in an Instagram bio | Mention only to sellers with a website |
| 0% commission for a year | Not relevant yet: we take no cut today | "When we start taking payments, founding creators pay 0% for the first year" |
| "Founding Unprompter" badge on the profile | **Not built** | Promise it, then build it before week 3, or drop it |
| Sharing without selling, from "Get started" | Built ("Share my work" on `/start`) | Send creators to unprompters.com/start |
| Featured spot on the home page | Not built as a feature | "I'll feature your work in our first spotlights" (social posts) |

## Who to message, and how many

- **Weeks 1–4:** illustrators and artists, then writers and poets. One or two scenes, so the first creators know each other.
- **Daily:** 20 new messages, plus follow-ups. Expect 1 in 5 to reply and 1 in 10 to post. 20 a day for four weeks is about 50–60 creators posting.
- **Pick people who:** post their process already (sketches, WIPs, drafts), have 500–20,000 followers (big enough to bring visitors, small enough to reply), and have posted in the last month. Bonus: anyone who's complained about AI or been accused of using it.

**Personalise the first line every time.** Name a specific piece and say what you liked about it. That one line is the difference between a reply and spam. Never send the same message twice in a row without changing it.

## 2. Follow-ups

**One follow-up only, 4–5 days later.** Then stop.

> Hi [name], just bumping this in case it got buried. No pressure at all. If you'd like to try it, I can set up your first post in about 10 minutes.

If they posted something new since, mention it instead: *"Just saw [new piece], gorgeous. Still happy to set you up on Unprompters if you'd like."*

## 3. When they say yes

Make it effortless. The goal is their first post live within 24 hours.

> Amazing, thank you! Here's how it works:
>
> 1. Go to unprompters.com/start and choose "Share my work". It takes about 2 minutes to answer three questions and make an account, and then you're on your first post.
> 2. Send me 1–3 pieces you'd like to share, plus any process you have: sketches, WIP shots, drafts, a time-lapse, raw footage. Whatever you'd normally keep in a folder.
> 3. I'll send you the title, description and answers for the "how was AI used" questions, ready to paste in. Or we can do it together on a 10-minute call.
>
> Once it's live, I'll share it from our account and add you to the founding creators' WhatsApp group.

After they post:
- Appreciate it and leave a real comment **the same day**.
- Ask them to share the link once: *"If you're happy with it, would you share the link in your story or bio? It helps people find the rest of the founding group."*
- Add them to the WhatsApp group (message in section 5).

## 4. Answers to common questions

**"Is this an AI detector?"**
> No. Detectors get things wrong, and they wrongly flag real people a lot. Your own answer about how AI was used is the starting point, your process evidence narrows it down, and scans only add a little and can never lower it. If anyone disagrees, a person reviews it, not a bot. The full method is public at unprompters.com/standard.

**"Do I have to sell anything?"**
> Not at all. Most people just share. If someone loves a piece, they can ask you whether you'd sell it, and you decide.

**"What does it cost? What's your cut?"**
> Free to post. If you sell, buyers pay you directly through your own payment link, and we take nothing. When we start handling payments ourselves, founding creators get the first year at 0%.

**"I don't have process evidence."**
> That's fine. You can post without it, and the score shows a wider range. Next time you make something, snap a few in-progress shots or keep your drafts. That's all it takes.

**"Will you use my work to train AI?"**
> Never. We don't train on anything posted, and we don't sell it to anyone who does. That's the point of the place.

**"Who are you? Why should I trust this?"**
> I'm [your name], building this from [city] because [one honest sentence: why you care]. It's early, which is why I'm inviting people personally. You can delete your posts at any time.

**"Was this site built with AI?"**
> Yes, I used AI tools to write the software. The promise is about the work people share, not the code behind the site. I'd rather say that upfront.

**"I use AI a little (for references, colour tests, editing)."**
> That's welcome. We're not anti-AI, we're honest about it. You say where it was used, and the page shows it. Lots of people use a bit.

## 8. Daily routine and what to track

**Every day (about an hour):**
- 20 new messages, personalised
- Follow up anyone from 4–5 days ago who hasn't replied
- Help everyone who said yes get their first post live
- Appreciate and comment on every new post on the site

**Every week:**
- Monday: post the weekly theme in the WhatsApp group
- Friday: one creator spotlight on Instagram and LinkedIn
- Update the numbers below

| Weekly number | Week 1 goal | Week 4 goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messages sent | 100 | 400 total |
| Replies | 20 | 80 total |
| Creators signed up | 10 | 60 total |
| Creators with a real work posted | 5 | 50 total |
| Works posted by other people | 10 | 120 total |
| Returning visitors (Vercel Analytics) | baseline | growing week over week |

If replies drop below 1 in 10, change the first message before sending more. Try a different opening line, a different group, or the "accused of AI" angle.

## Don'ts

- Don't paste the same message to many people. It's spam, it gets your account limited, and it's the fastest way to burn a small community.
- Don't post in subreddits or groups without reading their self-promotion rules.
- Don't repost anyone's work without asking.
- Don't call the score a certification or a guarantee. It's an estimate with a range.
- Don't promise features that aren't built (see the table at the top).
