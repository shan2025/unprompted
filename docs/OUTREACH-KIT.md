# Outreach kit: getting the first 100 creators

*Updated 16 November 2026 to match the live site. Messages, replies and posts for the founding-creator push in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Track every name in [outreach-tracker.csv](outreach-tracker.csv).*

**These are starting points, not scripts.** Put each one in your own words before you send it. You are inviting people to a place for human-made work, and a message that reads like a template is the fastest way to lose them. The first line, about their piece, must always be yours.

## Before you send anything

1. **Put real work on the site first.** Today it has three posts, all yours, and nothing for sale. Aim for 25–30 works from at least five people (friends, anyone who says yes early), each with one process image or draft. Every message sends people to the site, and an empty site wastes the invite. Demo content is now hidden on the live site automatically.
2. **Send from an account with real posts.** A blank or brand-new account looks like spam, and Instagram limits new accounts that send many similar messages. Your personal account, with a line in the bio about building Unprompters, works best.
3. **Know what to promise.** Only offer what is real today:

| Offer | Status | What to say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A page for each work, with your process attached and an AI score with its reasoning | Built | Say it freely |
| Free to post, no need to sell | Built | Say it freely |
| People can ask you about buying, even with no price | Built (Ask the creator) | Say it freely |
| Sell with your own payment link; we take nothing | Built. Buyer and seller arrange delivery themselves | Say it, and be clear there is no checkout or shipping on the site yet |
| AI training crawlers are told to stay away from your work | Built | "We tell AI training crawlers to stay away from work posted here. It's a request the big AI companies say they honour, not a guarantee." |
| "Founding Unprompter" badge on your profile and cards | Built. You switch it on from `/admin` | Promise it, and switch it on the day they post |
| Featured row on the home page | Built. You pick the work from `/admin` | "I'll feature your work on the home page" |
| An email when someone follows you, comments or appreciates a piece | Built | Say it freely |
| Free "Does my writing look AI-generated?" check | Built at unprompters.com/ai-writing-check | A good opener for writers and students |
| 0% commission for a year | Not relevant yet: we take no cut today | "When we start handling payments, founding creators pay 0% for the first year" |
| Embeddable score badge for your own website | Built for **items for sale only**, and it's HTML, so it can't go in an Instagram bio | Mention only to sellers with a website |
| Several images in one post | **Not built.** A post has one main image, plus process images attached as evidence | Don't promise a carousel |
| Hosted video or audio | **Not built.** Video is a YouTube or Vimeo link. There is no audio player | Tell musicians they need a video link for now |
| Checkout, shipping, reviews | **Not built** | Don't mention them |

**Words to avoid, because the site doesn't claim them:**
- "So nobody can call it AI" or "proof". Attached process is evidence people can look at, not proof. Say "so anyone can see how you made it".
- "Shown as a range". The score is one number with its reasoning.
- "Your evidence lowers your score". Evidence is shown publicly and can be reviewed by a moderator, but it does not change the number.
- "Certified", "verified human", "guaranteed".

## Who to message, and how many

- **Weeks 1–4:** illustrators and artists, then writers and poets. One or two scenes, so the first creators know each other.
- **Daily:** 20 new messages, plus follow-ups. Expect 1 in 5 to reply and 1 in 10 to post. 20 a day for four weeks is about 50–60 creators posting.
- **Pick people who:** already post their process (sketches, WIPs, drafts), have 500–20,000 followers (big enough to bring visitors, small enough to reply), and have posted in the last month. Bonus: anyone who has complained about AI or been accused of using it.
- **Where, in order:** Bluesky (artists who left X and Instagram over AI), Instagram and Threads, Reddit (replies only, see section 2), then email for people who list one.

**Personalise the first line every time.** Name a specific piece and say what you liked about it. That one line is the difference between a reply and spam.

## 2. Bluesky and Reddit: reply in public, don't cold-DM

### Bluesky reply (under an artist's process post; 300 characters max)

> This is lovely, the [detail] especially. I'm building a place where process like this sits next to the finished piece, for work made by people. Inviting the first 100 artists by hand. Can I send you the link?

### Bluesky post for the "no AI art" feeds

> I'm building Unprompters: a place to share work made by people, with your sketches and drafts next to the finished piece. Free, no need to sell anything, and AI training crawlers are told to stay away. Inviting the first 100 artists by hand. Reply and I'll send an invite.

### Reddit reply in an "I was accused of using AI" thread

Read the subreddit's self-promotion rules first. Help first, mention the site once, and don't link unless links are allowed.

> I'm sorry, this keeps happening to people who did the work. The thing that settles it is process, not a detector: drafts, sketches, version history. Keep everything from now on.
>
> Full disclosure, I'm building a site for this (Unprompters), where a piece is posted with its process attached. Happy to share the link if that's useful and allowed here.

### Cara

Don't recruit on Cara. Be a real member: post your own work, comment on others'. If someone asks what you're building, answer, and say people can post in both places.

## 4. When they say yes

Make it effortless. The goal is their first post live within 24 hours.

> Amazing, thank you! Here's how it works:
>
> 1. Go to unprompters.com/start and choose "Share my work". Three quick questions, an account, and you're on your first post.
> 2. Send me the piece you'd like to share first, plus any process you have: sketches, WIP shots, drafts, a time-lapse, raw footage. Whatever you'd normally keep in a folder.
> 3. I'll send back a title, a description and suggested answers for the "where was AI used" questions for you to check and paste in. Or we can do it together on a 10-minute call.
>
> Once it's live I'll add the Founding Unprompter badge to your profile and share the post from our account. You'll get an email whenever someone follows you, comments or appreciates it.

After they post:
- Switch on their Founding badge from `/admin` and feature the piece on the home page.
- Appreciate it and leave a real comment **the same day**.
- Ask them to share the link once: *"If you're happy with it, would you share the link in your story or bio? It helps people find the rest of the founding group."*
- Add them to the WhatsApp group (message in section 6).

## 5. Answers to common questions

**"Is this an AI detector?"**
> No. Detectors get things wrong and wrongly flag real people. Your own answer about where AI was used is the starting point. A scan can raise the score but never lower it, and it's never treated as a verdict. If there's a disagreement, a person looks at it. The full method is public at unprompters.com/standard.

**"Do I have to sell anything?"**
> Not at all. Most people just share. If someone loves a piece they can ask whether you'd sell it, and you decide.

**"What does it cost? What's your cut?"**
> Free to post. If you sell, buyers pay you directly through your own payment link and I take nothing. When the site starts handling payments itself, founding creators get the first year at 0%.

**"I don't have process evidence."**
> That's fine, you can post without it. The page will just say no evidence is attached. Next time you make something, snap a few in-progress shots or keep your drafts. That's all it takes.

**"Will you use my work to train AI?"**
> No. I don't train on anything posted and I don't sell it to anyone who does. The site also tells AI training crawlers, including OpenAI's, Anthropic's, Google's and Meta's, to stay away from work posted there. That's a request those companies say they honour. It isn't a lock against every scraper, and I won't pretend it is.

**"Who are you? Why should I trust this?"**
> I'm [your name], building this from [city] because [one honest sentence: why you care]. It's early, which is why I'm inviting people one at a time. You can delete your posts whenever you like. There's more at unprompters.com/about.

**"Was this site built with AI?"**
> Yes, I used AI tools to write the software, and the About page says so. The promise is about the work people share, not the code behind the site.

**"I use AI a little (for references, colour tests, editing)."**
> You're welcome here. It's not anti-AI, it's honest about it. You say where you used it and the page shows that. Work that's mostly AI-made isn't accepted.

**"Can I post more than one image?"**
> One main image per post for now, plus your process images attached underneath. Multiple images per post is on my list.

## 9. Promoting the free writing check

The check at unprompters.com/ai-writing-check is the easiest thing on the site to share: it's free, needs no account and helps someone in two minutes. Use it to bring people in. Most of them will be students and writers, not creators who post, so treat it as reach and keep the creator invites going alongside.

**Say:** free, no sign-up, nothing is uploaded, it shows *why* a passage stands out, and it doesn't give a verdict.

**Never say:** "check if your essay will pass Turnitin", "avoid AI detection", "beat the detector" or "100% accurate". None is true, and the first three are the opposite of what the site stands for.

### Reddit reply, in a "my essay was flagged as AI" thread

Good places: r/college, r/GradSchool, r/Teachers, r/freelanceWriters. Read each one's rules first. Help first, and mention the tool once.

> That's stressful, I'm sorry. Detectors react to style, not to who wrote it, so careful formal writing gets flagged a lot. What helps most is your process: version history in Google Docs or Word, notes, an outline. Offer to walk your instructor through it.
>
> If you want to see which passages a detector is reacting to, I made a free check that shows them and says why. It runs in your browser, so your essay isn't uploaded anywhere. Disclosure: it's my project. Happy to share the link if that's allowed here.

### Class or student group message (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord)

> If you've ever worried your own essay might get flagged as AI: this is a free check that shows which parts a detector reacts to and why. No sign-up, and your text doesn't leave your phone. It isn't Turnitin and it won't tell you if you'll "pass", but it's useful to see before you hand something in.
> unprompters.com/ai-writing-check

### 30-second video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok)

Screen recording, no face needed. One take.

1. **Text on screen:** "Wrote it yourself and it still got flagged as AI?"
2. Open the page and tap **Fill in an example**, then **Check my writing**.
3. Let the percentage count up, then scroll to the highlighted passage and pause on **Why it stands out**.
4. **Say or caption:** "It gives you a percentage and shows you why, passage by passage. Free, no sign-up, and your essay never leaves your phone."
5. **End card:** unprompters.com/ai-writing-check

It doesn't give a verdict and shouldn't be used to accuse anyone. Nothing pasted into it is uploaded.

## 10. Daily routine and what to track

**Every day (about an hour):**
- 20 new messages, each with its own first line
- Follow up anyone from 4–5 days ago who hasn't replied
- Help everyone who said yes get their first post live
- Appreciate and comment on every new post on the site

**Every week:**
- Monday: post the weekly theme in the WhatsApp group
- Friday: feature three works on the home page, and one creator spotlight on Instagram and LinkedIn
- Update the numbers below

| Weekly number | Week 1 goal | Week 4 goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messages sent | 100 | 400 total |
| Replies | 20 | 80 total |
| Creators signed up | 10 | 60 total |
| Creators with a real work posted | 5 | 50 total |
| Works posted by other people | 10 | 120 total |
| Returning visitors (Vercel Analytics) | baseline | growing week over week |

If replies drop below 1 in 10, change the first message before sending more. Try a different opening line, a different group, or the "accused of AI" angle.

## Don'ts

- Don't paste the same message to many people. It's spam, it gets your account limited, and it's the fastest way to burn a small community.
- Don't post in subreddits or groups without reading their self-promotion rules.
- Don't repost anyone's work without asking.
- Don't call the score a certification, a proof or a guarantee. It's an estimate, not a measurement.
- Don't promise features that aren't built (see the table at the top).
